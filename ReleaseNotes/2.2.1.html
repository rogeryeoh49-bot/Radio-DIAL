Radio Dial 2.2.1

  • Self Radio follows audio from other apps and can hand playback back to Apple Music or Spotify. Supported players can also supply track details and artwork. System audio detection requires macOS 14.2 or later.
  • A guided setup walks through the player, VI Radio shortcuts and optional permissions. Replay it from Settings.
  • Cover Flow improves station-logo framing, artwork caching and transitions when playback changes.
  • Radio Dial handles station announcements and incomplete track metadata more reliably, and checks song and artist matches before using catalog artwork.
