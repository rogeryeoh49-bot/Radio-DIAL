Radio Dial 2.2.0 — VI radio

Radio Dial 2.2 introduces VI radio, a global radio selector inspired by GTA VI. Hold Option + Q to bring your stations onto the screen, browse a row of station logos, and keep listening without returning to the main player.

VI radio — inspired by GTA VI

  • A new full-screen overlay with a horizontal station carousel, a bright selection border, condensed typography and smooth sliding transitions. Existing themes remain available.
  • Hold Option + Q to show the selector; by default, each station starts playing as you select it. Release the shortcut to close the overlay and keep the selected station playing.
  • Choose confirmation mode if you prefer to browse first: Space, Return or clicking a logo starts playback. Releasing the opening shortcut closes an unconfirmed preview.
  • Prefer a single press? Toggle mode opens on the first press and closes on the second. The opening shortcut and previous/next keys can be customized.
  • Browse with arrow keys, scrolling, Tab for next, or the backtick key above Tab for previous. Shift-Tab also goes back. Caps Lock remains available as a custom binding.
  • Favorites come first and have a gold star beside the station name. A loading spinner appears on the opposite side while a stream connects; the name stays centered.
  • Station logos preload around the selection and stay cached between openings. Song titles and artists appear on separate lines with matching typography.
  • A short selection sound replaces continuous loading static within VI radio. Trackpad haptics mark each station change, with stronger feedback for favorites.
  • Turn Off pauses playback and closes the selector. Escape closes it without undoing stations already played in immediate mode.
  • Key hints stay hidden until 8 seconds pass without a key press, click or scroll. Moving the pointer does not restart the timer.
  • Open VI radio from the View menu or its card in Themes, and manage it in its own settings tab. The native global shortcut needs no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.

Make the player yours

  • Settings → Shortcuts now lets you change Play/Pause, Stop, Recording, Volume Up, Volume Down, Mute and Mini Player bindings. Each has its own Reset button; Reset All restores the defaults.
  • Playback menu shortcuts update with your choices. Conflicting bindings are flagged, and Cover Flow uses your chosen Play/Pause key for the centered station.
  • Settings → General adds a saved default station country, initially All Countries. The player restores the last source, with automatic playback controlled by Resume last source on launch.
  • VI radio settings have independent Reset buttons for the opening shortcut and both navigation keys, with shorter instructions.
